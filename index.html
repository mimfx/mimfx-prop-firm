GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
make your move.

Clear answers are part of a better trading experience.

Can I buy a challenge right now?+

No. This is an early website prototype. Checkout, account provisioning, and challenge operations are not active.

Can I log in to a real MT5 account here?+

Not yet. A compatible MT5 broker or server provider and secure integration must be selected and tested before live account access can be offered.

Are the account balances and chart live?+

No. All balances, targets, and chart graphics on this page are illustrative. No live market feed or trading account is connected.

Will passing a challenge guarantee a payout?+

No payout offer or guarantee is made by this prototype. Any future evaluation and reward programme will need clear terms, operational funding, and appropriate legal review.
