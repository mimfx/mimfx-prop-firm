THE DETAILS MATTER

Questions before
you begin.

We believe trust starts with honest answers and clear expectations.

Can I purchase a challenge now?＋

Not yet. This is a front-end prototype. Checkout, account provisioning and challenge operations are not active.

Can I connect a real MT5 account?＋

Not yet. A compatible MT5 broker or server provider and secure integration must be selected, built and tested first.

Are the balances and performance chart live?＋

No. All balances, targets and chart graphics shown here are illustrative preview data.

Are payouts currently available?＋

Planned simulated-funded trader profit share: 70%. Nexora intends to complete payout verification and payment within 7 calendar days after a request, using its own available funds. Each evaluation phase has a 60-calendar-day deadline from activation; failing to pass within that period results in a breach. These policies are not active yet; no payout or challenge service is currently connected.
