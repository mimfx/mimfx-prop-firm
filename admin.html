Risk engine foundation: daily drawdown is equity-based and resets at 00:00 UTC, using the account's equity at the start of each UTC day. 2-Phase static maximum drawdown is equity-based: floating losses count against the fixed initial-balance floor. 1-Phase trailing maximum drawdown uses CLOSED BALANCE only: realized gains can raise the floor, floating profit cannot, and losses never lower it.
